A LITTLE LETTER

NADA,

I don't think a birthday message could ever hold everything I feel for you, but I still wanted to make you a little world that says it for me.

Thank you for every laugh, every tiny moment, every memory, and every version of yourself you have shared with me.

You make ordinary days feel like something worth keeping.

I want you to know that I truly see you as one of the best people in the whole world, and to me, you are the best and most beautiful engineer I could ever know. I hope you always believe in yourself, keep going after what you want, and achieve everything you dream of.

Today is about you. I hope you always know how loved, appreciated, and wonderfully special you are.

Forever yours ♡
